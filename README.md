# 🏥 Hospital ER Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Hospital ER Analytics Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze Emergency Room patient activity, operational performance, wait times, referrals, demographics, and patient satisfaction.

The dashboard transforms patient-level data into an interactive management reporting tool that helps identify patient volume patterns, operational bottlenecks, and areas that may require further attention.

The project focuses on answering a practical business question:

> **How can Emergency Room data be used to understand patient demand, operational performance, and patient experience?**

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to help answer the following questions:

- How many patients are visiting the Emergency Room?
- What percentage of patients are admitted versus released?
- When are patient volumes highest?
- Which shifts experience longer waiting times?
- Which age groups represent the largest patient volume?
- Which departments generate the most referrals?
- How satisfied are patients with their experience?
- How does patient volume vary by month, day, and shift?
- Which operational patterns could help management improve ER performance?

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

Provides a high-level view of Emergency Room performance through:

- Total Patients
- Admitted Patients
- Released Patients
- Average Wait Time
- Average Satisfaction
- Admission Rate
- Gender Distribution
- Patient Volume Trends

### 2. Patient Analysis

Examines patient demographics and characteristics, including:

- Age Group
- Gender
- Patient volume
- Referral information
- Patient distribution

### 3. Operational Performance

Focuses on ER efficiency and demand patterns:

- Average Wait Time
- Wait Time Distribution
- Patient Volume by Shift
- Patient Volume by Day
- Monthly Patient Volume
- Weekly wait-time patterns

### 4. Patient Satisfaction

Analyzes patient experience using:

- Average Satisfaction
- Satisfaction Rating Distribution
- High / Moderate / Low Satisfaction
- Satisfaction by Age Group
- Satisfaction by Referral Department
- Satisfaction by Patient Race
- Wait Time by Shift

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

### Patient Volume

Patient volume varies considerably across months and shifts, allowing management to identify periods of increased demand.

### Operational Performance

Average wait time is approximately **35 minutes**, with differences across shifts and days of the week.

The analysis also shows that night-shift operations can experience higher waiting times across several days.

### Patient Demographics

The dashboard segments patients by age group and other demographic attributes to identify which populations account for the largest share of ER visits.

### Patient Satisfaction

The satisfaction dashboard evaluates overall patient experience and identifies differences in satisfaction across age groups, referral departments, and demographic segments.

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard demonstrates how healthcare organizations can use operational data to:

- Monitor ER demand
- Identify high-volume periods
- Understand waiting-time patterns
- Evaluate patient satisfaction
- Monitor admission performance
- Support staffing and resource planning
- Identify operational areas for further investigation
